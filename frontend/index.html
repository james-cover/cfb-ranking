How the rankings work

AP Poll is the published ranking. Predicted AP estimates how voters will rank teams. Independent model is the XGBoost strength ranking built from performance and résumé data.

The team page shows per-team feature contributions, such as SHAP values. These are not fixed polynomial weights; they show which inputs pushed that team's output up or down.

Game edge is the difference between the model margin and market spread from the listed favorite's perspective.
