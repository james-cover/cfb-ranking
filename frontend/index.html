How the rankings work

AP Poll is the published ranking. Predicted AP estimates how voters will rank teams. Independent model is the XGBoost strength ranking built from performance and résumé data.

The team page shows grouped TreeSHAP contributions. They are not fixed polynomial weights; they show which inputs pushed that team's model rating up or down.

Moneyline EV combines the model win probability with the best currently available sportsbook payout. An EV of +$12 means an estimated $12 average profit per $100 wager over many identical opportunities, not a guaranteed return.
